Smart Embedded Monitoring for Urban Water bodies

This project is developed under the Department of Information Technology for the Sabarmati and is designed to scale to urban water bodies across Gujarat and India.

Department of Information Technology
B.Tech Semester 8 · Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India

Primary Deployment Site
Sabarmati Riverfront, Ahmedabad — 22 km stretch

Partners & Stakeholders
AMC · SRFDCL · Smart Cities Mission · NMCG

Technology Stack
YOLOv8 · Django · IoT · GIS · LSTM · AWS

 Research Interest

The integration of YOLOv8-based floating trash detection with GIS-tagged event logging for Indian urban riverfronts is a novel contribution with significant publication potential.
