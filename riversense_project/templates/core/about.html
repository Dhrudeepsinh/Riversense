Mission

Why We Built RiverSense and focus on Sabarmati

The Sabarmati Riverfront spans 22 kilometres of Ahmedabad's riverbank and hosts millions of visitors annually. Yet floating plastics, solid waste clusters, and sewage inflows remain a persistent challenge that conventional periodic inspections cannot address in real time.

We identified a critical research gap: no integrated, deployable system exists specifically for Indian urban riverfronts that combines visual trash detection, IoT water quality monitoring and GIS visualisation in a single platform.

RiverSense directly aligns with India's Smart Cities Mission, AMRUT 2.0, Swachh Bharat Abhiyan and the NMCG framework — designed to scale from the Sabarmati to every river city in India.

Problem Identification

Surveyed Sabarmati Riverfront management gaps — waste detection, water quality, flood prediction — all addressed in isolation, never together.

Dataset Curation

Curated and annotated 801 training images of water surface waste using Roboflow.

YOLOv8 Training

Trained YOLOv8-medium for 150 epochs on Tesla T4 GPU. Achieved mAP@0.5 = 0.849 and Precision = 0.969. No overfitting observed.

Dashboard Development

Built full Django dashboard with live sensor data, GIS detection heatmap, user login, flood alerts and incident management.

Scalability Design

Architected for Gujarat-wide deployment — Kankaria, Vastrapur, Chandola lakes and beyond. National NMCG compatibility built in.
